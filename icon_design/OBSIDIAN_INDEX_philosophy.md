# OBSIDIAN INDEX
### A visual philosophy of luminous order set into darkness

---

**Obsidian Index** is the art of the precious thing placed with intent. It begins with a single dark field — a deep lacquered void, not flat black but a navy so deep it remembers light — and into that field it sets a small parliament of glowing forms. The void is never empty; it is a vault, a setting, a held breath of space whose only purpose is to make what rests inside it feel chosen, finite, and rare. Nothing floats. Everything is *placed*, the way a jeweler seats a stone or a printer beds a slug of type into a case, and the placing must read as the work of a hand that has done this ten thousand times.

In **space and form**, the movement is governed by the squircle — the superelliptic corner that is neither circle nor square, the most patiently resolved curve in all of geometry. The outer vessel is a single rounded monolith, generous and calm, and within it the contents observe a quiet quadrant order: a two-by-two field where each member holds equal weight and equal silence. Margins are wide and deliberate. Negative space is not leftover; it is structural, the dark mortar between luminous bricks. The forms sit slightly proud of their bed, lifted by shadow, so the eye reads depth before it reads color — a recessed world that nonetheless leans toward the light.

The **color and material** are jewel and lacquer. Each luminous form carries its own vertical gradient, lit from above as though a single cool sun hangs just out of frame: a warm amber that behaves like polished brass, a cobalt that deepens to ink, a teal cut like sea-glass, a violet that smolders toward its base. These are not decorative hues but *materials* — each surface believes it has weight, gloss, and a faint top sheen where light grazes its upper edge. Against the obsidian field the chroma sings without shouting; restraint is the amplifier. The palette is closed, calibrated, and cohesive, the result of painstaking tuning where a single wrong value would break the spell.

In **scale, rhythm, and light**, the composition rewards the long look. A whisper-thin highlight rides the top lip of the vessel; a soft shadow pools at its foot; within, each form repeats the same lit-from-above logic so the whole reads as one coherent optical event rather than four separate marks. This internal rhyme — the same gesture performed four times with perfect consistency — is the signature of master-level execution. The piece must look meticulously crafted at every magnification: monumental and architectural when large, legible and unmistakable when reduced to a thumbnail, never losing its poise.

**Visual hierarchy** flows from darkness to light to detail. First the silhouette, then the glow, then — only on close inspection — the bevel, the grain of the lacquer, the hairline rim where each surface meets the void. Text, if it appears at all, is a single clinical whisper: a coordinate, a name, a registration mark set in the cool monospace of an instrument panel, never a sentence, never an explanation. The image carries the meaning; the words only certify it.

Above all, **Obsidian Index** is a discipline of craftsmanship over decoration. The goal is not ornament but inevitability — a composition so carefully balanced, so painstakingly lit and seated and refined, that it appears to have always existed in exactly this form. Every radius, every gradient stop, every soft shadow is the product of deep expertise and countless quiet refinements. It should feel labored over with love and finished by someone at the absolute top of their field: pristine, cohesive, and quietly, undeniably masterful.
